🎉 Tic-Tac-Toe Party — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Tic-Tac-Toe Party is a pass-the-phone game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and what happens when you use the optional Pro purchase features.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game information locally on your device so it can remember your progress between sessions. Depending on how you use the app, this can include:

  • player names and local player profiles;
  • game results, win/loss/draw records, streaks, head-to-head records, series, tournaments, weekly challenges, and badges;
  • your settings, such as themes, sound, music, and AI difficulty;
  • the local status of one-time sample games; and
  • a local cache of your Pro entitlement status.

Player names can be personal information if you choose to enter a real person's name. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer. The app does not have a developer-operated account system, database, or game server.

Backups and clipboard data

The Settings screen lets you export your locally stored game data as a backup. When you choose this option, the app copies the backup text to your device clipboard. You can also restore a backup by pasting text into the app. The app does not upload backups to the developer. Clipboard contents may be accessible to the operating system or other apps according to your device settings, so do not share a backup that contains information you want to keep private. Portable backups exclude Game Center player identifiers, leaderboard totals, and pending Game Center reports.

In-app purchases

Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives product information and the purchase or entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple ID password. Apple may process transaction, account, device, and payment information under Apple's Privacy Policy and the terms that apply to your Apple Account.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account and the App Store subscription settings. The app includes a Restore Purchases option for eligible Apple transactions.

Game Center (optional)

Leaderboards, achievements, and friend notifications are provided by Apple's Game Center. When Game Center is enabled and signed in, qualifying daily-completion totals and achievements are submitted to Apple under your Game Center account. The app receives your Game Center display alias and an Apple-scoped player identifier. It keeps the scoped identifier locally so queued reports cannot cross accounts; the alias is used only to reflect the current sign-in state and is not sent to the developer. Pass-the-phone player names and results are not submitted to Game Center. Apple's handling of Game Center information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

The app does not use analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect or receive your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, messages, browsing history, player names, or gameplay history. Apple receives only the purchase and Game Center data described above under its own services. We do not knowingly collect information through the app for advertising or marketing. The app has no developer-operated online account or backend service.

Third-party services and links

Apple's StoreKit is used for Pro purchases and subscription entitlement checks. Apple's GameKit is used, only when you opt in via Game Center, for leaderboards and achievements. Apple handles the information required for those services under its own privacy practices.

The app may provide links to this policy, Apple's standard Licensed Application EULA, and Apple's websites. When you open an external link, your device connects to the linked website, which may process standard technical information such as an IP address and browser or device details under that provider's privacy policy. The developer does not control those websites.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may preserve that data. Any backup text that you copied or saved outside the app must be deleted separately from the clipboard or location where you saved it.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain a server-side copy of this game data, there is no developer account or server record to delete. For Apple purchase records and subscription data, use Apple's account and subscription controls.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children. Game data entered into the app remains locally on the device as described above.

Security

The app relies on the security protections provided by your device and operating system for locally stored data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting any backup you export from the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. You should review this page periodically.
